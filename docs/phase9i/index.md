## Maturity gate

The phase remains active until at least **30 completed non-overlapping candidate trades** or **90 calendar days after the frozen cutoff with sufficient completed observations**.

A run with no completed new trades is recorded as a **data-readiness state**, not as evidence for or against the strategy.

## Deployment boundary

Phase 9I does **not** authorize paper or live trading merely because a fresh holdout is positive. Any later promotion requires a separate implementation/paper-trading gate and explicit review of fresh chronological evidence, costs, stability and uncertainty.
